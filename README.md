# FAB Mobile Banking Login in the UAE: Sign In and Check Your Balance

By Daniel Collins

Mobile banking gives FAB customers a fast way to sign in and check a balance from a phone. This guide explains the login steps, the details you may need, and what to do when sign in does not work as expected.

## Login steps in the app

1. Open the official FAB mobile banking app on your phone.
2. Enter your username or customer ID as requested.
3. Enter your password carefully, checking for extra spaces or capital letters.
4. Complete any one-time code or approval step shown on the screen.
5. Wait for the home screen to load, then choose the account you want to review.

## What details you need

- Your registered username, customer ID or mobile number, depending on how your account was set up.
- Your password and access to the phone or email used for security codes.
- A stable internet connection and the latest version of the app.

Keep these details private. The bank will not ask you to share a full password or one-time code by message or call.

## Fixing login problems

- If the password is rejected, reset it through the official forgot password option in the app.
- If a code does not arrive, check your signal, wait a minute, and request a new code once.
- If the app will not open, update it, restart your phone, and try again.
- If your account appears locked, follow the in-app recovery steps or contact the bank through its official support channel.

## Checking balance after login

After sign in, open your account from the home screen. The available balance is the amount you can use now. Recent payments may still be pending, so compare the balance with the transaction list before you assume a charge is missing or wrong.

## Security tips

- Use a screen lock and keep your phone software updated.
- Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared device.
- Do not sign in through links sent in unexpected messages.
- Review transactions regularly and report anything unfamiliar promptly.
